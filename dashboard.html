Methodology & Limitations

Data Source
Government of India Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in)
CPCB CAAQMS Real-time Air Quality Index
Resource ID: 3b01bcb8-0b14-4abf-b6f2-c1bfd384ba69
Snapshot
23 September 2026, 12:00:00 IST
Single point-in-time snapshot. No temporal trends, seasonality, or changes over time can be established from this dataset.
AQI Calculation
All AQI values are Calculated AQI, not official CPCB-reported AQI.
Method: CPCB 2014 National AQI Framework — piecewise linear sub-index formula, composite = max sub-index.
Averaging Period
The pollutant_avg field's averaging window is not documented in the CSV.
Assumed: 24-hour average for PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, NH3; 8-hour average for CO and OZONE — consistent with CPCB 2014 methodology.
CO Unit
CO values in dataset are in µg/m³ (confirmed by physical plausibility — mg/m³ interpretation would place all stations in the Severe category, which is physically impossible).
Converted to mg/m³ (÷1000) before applying CPCB AQI sub-index breakpoints.
Data Quality
343 NA readings (9.8% of 3,507 records) retained and flagged.
13 suspicious zero readings (NH3=11, SO2=2) retained and flagged as "requires validation".
3 possible sensor anomalies (CO flat readings >100 µg/m³) retained and flagged.
AQI Confidence
Full (A): 359 stations — all 7 sub-indexes available.
Partial-High (B): 110 stations — 4–6 sub-indexes.
Partial-Low (C): 26 stations — 1–3 sub-indexes.
Single (C): 6 stations — 1 sub-index only.
Coverage
Monitoring coverage is highly unequal. Top 5 states hold 55.5% of all stations. 9 states/UTs have 1 station each. AQI comparisons across states must account for this imbalance.
Standardization
Normalized pollutant profiles use city median ÷ national 75th percentile.
Diversity (entropy) uses Shannon entropy of pollutant sub-index profile per station.
Z-scores used for clustering comparison only — not for AQI calculation.
Analytical Scope
All associations in this project are observational. No causal claims are made. Cross-sectional data cannot establish pollution trends, causes, or changes over time.
